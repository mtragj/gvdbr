This 45 mile loop begins at Third Flats Trailhead on Second Thoughts, followed by Butterknife, Canyon View, Rampage, Saddle Up, Pony Tail and Lasso. The loop finishes with Windmill single track, Tabaguache trail and Bangs Canyon Jeep Road. This route includes ledges, drops, fast, flowing switchbacks, some exposure, rocks and trees.

Riders should have solid advanced skills and good riding endurance. There will be scheduled rest stops to take in the views and have a snack, with an extended break at the Gunnison River. The time on trail will be approximately 4 hours. Enduro bikes with low gearing, electric start and tubliss or mousse tubes is highly recommended.
