We're excited to announce this year's door prizes for the 2026 Grand Valley Dirt Bike Rally!

**Grand Prize:** A brand new **2024 GASGAS Moto 1 E-Bike**, a **$2,599 MSRP** value, donated by [Teddy Morse's Grand Junction Powersports](https://www.gjktm.com/).

**Runner Up:** A **$250 [Klim](https://www.klim.com/) gift card**.

## How to Enter

- **Every ride you sign up for is one entry.** Ride Saturday *and* Sunday to double your chances!
- Entry is automatic with registration, so there are no extra tickets or forms.
- The drawing will be held **Saturday, October 17th at 6:00 PM** at the [27-1/4 Road MX Track event area](https://maps.app.goo.gl/eYvQMGQFdTL6SLk26).
- **You must be present to win!**

Stick around after your ride for the drawing. The food truck, vendor expo, and Slow Ride Contest will all be going that evening, so check out the [activities schedule]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/activities/) for the full weekend lineup.

## Thank You to Our Sponsors

A huge thank you to Teddy Morse's Grand Junction Powersports and Klim for their generous support of this year's Rally. Sponsors like these help the MTRA keep the Rally going and support our work on local trails. Please thank them with your business!
